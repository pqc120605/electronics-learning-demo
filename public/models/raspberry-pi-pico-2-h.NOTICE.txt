The GLB is a local conversion of Raspberry Pi's STEP source using the project's
OpenCascade-based converter. Geometry is grouped by source CAD names and placed
in the viewer coordinate frame. The viewer applies educational display colors;
it does not add electrical connectivity, pin behavior, or firmware simulation.
