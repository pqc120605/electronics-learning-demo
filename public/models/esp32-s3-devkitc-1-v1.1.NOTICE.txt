Accuracy and safety
- This model was derived from imported PADS ASCII/BOM data and has not been checked against a physical board.
- The PADS-to-KiCad import has DRC findings and unconnected items. This model is not suitable for PCB fabrication, electrical sign-off, or as a validated netlist.
- Generic package mesh, imported net labels, and the 44 selectable header anchors are educational reference data. Confirm exact revision, module variant, datasheet, voltage limits, and wiring before hardware use.
- Outline height from the source PADS conversion is 62.865 mm, differing by 0.125 mm from the official dimension PDF (62.74 mm); the converted source outline is kept aligned to the imported PCB geometry.
