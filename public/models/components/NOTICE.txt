Mạch Mở component models

Files listed as "source-cad-mesh" in src/data/component-models.manifest.json are adapted from the KiCad 3D model
library (kicad-packages3D, shipped with KiCad 9.0) by the KiCad Libraries contributors:
https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D
License: CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Terms: https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/
Changes: STEP tessellated to GLB, rotated to Y-up, scaled to metres. These adapted files
remain under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Files listed as "parametric-mesh" were generated by scripts/generate-component-models.mjs
as educational approximations. They are not manufacturer CAD.

capacitor-ceramic.glb and neopixel.glb (added 2026-11-14) are adapted from the KiCad 3D model library
shipped with KiCad 10.0.7 (Capacitor_THT.3dshapes/C_Disc_D5.0mm_W2.5mm_P5.00mm.step and
LED_SMD.3dshapes/LED_WS2812B_PLCC4_5.0x5.0mm_P3.2mm.step) by the KiCad Libraries contributors.
License: CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes: STEP tessellated to GLB, rotated to Y-up, scaled to metres.

Files listed as "kicad-assembly" (added 2026-11-14) are module boards assembled by
scripts/build-module-models.py with KiCad 10.0.7: PCB outline, soldermask colour, silkscreen text
and footprints from the KiCad libraries, exported with kicad-cli and post-processed by
scripts/finalize-module-glb.mjs. The package bodies come from the KiCad 3D model library
(kicad-packages3D, KiCad Libraries contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/).
Parts with no library model (ultrasonic transducers, display glass, heatsink, PIR lens, DHT22
housing, encoder body, AA cells) are simple shapes from scripts/make-primitive-part-glb.mjs.
These derived files remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. Layouts follow common hobby modules; they are not
manufacturer CAD.

potentiometer, thermistor, servo, dc-motor, limit-switch, jumper, power-bank, usb-cable and
soil-relay-pump were rebuilt on 2026-11-14 by scripts/build-detailed-parts.mjs as detailed
parametric shapes (no openly licensed model was found). They are educational approximations.
