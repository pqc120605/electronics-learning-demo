Modifications
- The board GLB was exported with KiCad CLI from the v1.3 PCB source.
- 47 front-side component footprints were given standard KiCad 3D package shapes matched by footprint family. These package meshes are reference shapes at package-family level, not exact manufacturer-part models, markings, or verified component builds.
- 68 PCB footprints are included in the selectable inventory. The BOM has 67 component entries; the additional back-side U$2 item is a Seeed logo graphic, not an assembled part.
- Four populated footprint families without a verified shape (button, PMOS, USB-C connector, and X4 crystal) are listed without a fabricated mesh. DNP footprints are also identified and not rendered as installed parts.
- Source hashes and the generation script are in the project inventory and `scripts/generate-seeed-xiao-esp32c3-model.mjs`.

Limitations
This is a browser reference model, not a physical scan, manufacturing model, or fit-check. Board revision has not been checked against a physical sample. The model has no pin picking, net coloring, firmware execution, or electrical simulation. Consult the source schematic and datasheets before wiring hardware.
