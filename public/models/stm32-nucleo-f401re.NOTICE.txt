What this is
- A board assembled with KiCad 10.0.7 by scripts/build-stm32-education-boards.py from KiCad standard
  library footprints and 3D package models, then exported with kicad-cli (pcb export glb).
- Board size, header pitch/row spacing and pin order follow public documentation
  (Blue Pill: stm32-base.org; Nucleo-64: ST UM1724). Positions of secondary parts are approximate.
- It is NOT vendor CAD and NOT a scan of a physical board. Do not use it for fabrication or wiring sign-off.
- ST's MB1136 design files were not used: their licence (ST OPLA) does not clear redistribution.
- The Arduino-compatible connector geometry (CN5/CN6/CN8/CN9) is copied from the Arduino Uno Rev3 CAD,
  © Arduino, CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This derivative keeps that licence.
