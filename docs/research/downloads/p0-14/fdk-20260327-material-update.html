Mass production and shipment of PFAS-free(*1) nickel-metal hydride batteries begins
- By adopting new materials, we contribute to reducing environmental impact and building a sustainable society!

March 27 2026
FDK CORPORATION

FDK CORPORATION (President and CEO: Ryo Nagano) will upgrade its nickel-metal hydride batterymodels HR-3UTG (AA-size) and HR-4UTG (AAA-size). Mass production and shipments will begin from April 2026.

Overview

Since 1991 and over 35 years, FDK has been manufacturing and selling nickel metal hydride batteries for various applications such as security, automotive, medical, and home appliances, with prominent features including "usable in a wide temperature range," "rechargeable and reusable," "highly recyclable (*2)" and "highly safe (*3).


Recent trends show increasing efforts to regulate PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances). PFAS are widely used in everything from daily necessities to industrial products due to their water- and oil-repellent properties.

However, they are difficult to break down in nature and are said to potentially accumulate gradually in the bodies of humans and animals, raising concerns about their impact on the environment and health.

PFAS are also widely used as a key material to stabilize the performance and lifetime of secondary batteries, including our nickel-metal hydride batteries. FDK engineers, driven by a desire to provide more environmentally friendly batteries, undertook development work with extremely high technical challenges to eliminate PFAS from flagship models.

As a result, by replacing PFAS with new materials and applying a new manufacturing process, we succeeded in developing PFAS-free nickel-metal hydride batteries (HR-3UTG/HR-4UTG) that maintain the same battery characteristics as current batteries.


By consistently supplying nickel-metal hydride batteries that reduce environmental impact throughout their entire lifecycle ― from manufacturing to disposal (recycling) ―　FDK aim to contribute to a sustainable society.


Mass production and shipment of these products will begin from April 2026.


All FDK nickel-metal hydride batteries are made in FDK's Japan domestic factory which is ISO9001 / ISO14001 certified and complies with necessary environmental requirements such as RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances Directive) and the EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542).

FDK, as a Smart Energy Partner, will continue to provide customers with solutions for the efficient use of electric energy in a safe and efficient manner by assembling advanced technologies.
